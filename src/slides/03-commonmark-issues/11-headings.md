notes: |
  ATX, ATX with closing hashes, and setext. CommonMark reads all three as the same h1.
  Setext also makes `---` mean two things: an h2 underline or a thematic break.

```markdown
# Heading

# Heading #

Heading
=======
```

Three headings. All the same `<h1>`.
